Standard check-in and check-out times are confirmed at booking — message us on your preferred dates and we'll walk you through it.

The downpayment is non-refundable. Rebooking is allowed up to 7 days before your check-in date.

No — Nap & Yap Staycation is a strictly no-smoking unit.
